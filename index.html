~ our story ~

A family kitchen. A whole town's favorite morning.

We didn't set out to win awards. We set out to feed people the way our family was fed — warm coffee in real mugs, eggs cooked the way you actually wanted them, and a slice of Pat's cobbler if you stayed long enough to deserve it.

Twelve years later, we've been named Best Breakfast in California, taken home a wall full of Fresno Bee People's Choice awards, and still know most of our regulars by their usual order. None of that changes what happens at 6 AM: the grill turns on, the biscuits go in, and Fresno starts its day.

— The Train Depot Family
